# SAT Reading Hacks

The video presents a repeatable APE loop: Analyze the passage or sentence for context; Predict what a correct answer should say; Eliminate choices that do not match the prediction or the text. It then applies that loop by question type.

## Type-specific operating rules

- Inference: draw only what follows from the stated facts; do not import outside knowledge.
- Textual evidence: pair the conclusion with the line that directly supports it, not a merely related detail.
- Cross-text connections: summarize each text, state the relationship between them, and select the conclusion supported by both.
- Purpose/structure: identify what the sentence or paragraph is doing in the argument, not only what information it contains.
- Central ideas: distinguish the passage's controlling claim from an illustrative detail.
- Words in context: predict the role/meaning from sentence logic before letting an unfamiliar option drive the interpretation.

The common discipline is prediction before choices. This works with [[summaries/settele-dumb-summaries]] and the strong-word/trap-answer checks in [[concepts/sat-hard-reading-strategy-stack]].
