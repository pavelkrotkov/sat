# Dumb Summaries

Core move: simplify every passage, question, answer choice, chart, and vocabulary item to its essential logical content. The goal is not to understand every technical noun; it is to preserve the relationship being tested while discarding distracting detail.

## Procedure

1. Read for the passage's basic situation, claim, change, or disagreement.
2. Translate dense material into ordinary language or variables (X causes Y; X challenges Y).
3. Reduce each answer choice to its claim and compare that claim with the question's target.
4. Keep the summary flexible: revise it when a later sentence changes the relationship.
5. For evidence questions, use the simplified claim to locate the choice that directly supports it.

## High-score use

This is a compression tool, not permission to ignore qualifying language. Preserve negation, comparison, scope, and causal direction; simplify nouns before simplifying logic.
