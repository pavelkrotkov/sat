# SAT Hard-Question Strategy Stack

For a 750-to-800 Reading and Writing goal, the shared method across these videos is controlled reduction followed by proof checking:

1. Analyze the question and passage; name the relationship being tested.
2. Dumb it down into a short logical skeleton while preserving direction, scope, and negation.
3. Predict the answer before reading choices when possible.
4. Inspect each choice as a compound claim.
5. Locate the exact support and penalize any unsupported strong word, leap, reversal, irrelevant truth, or neighboring-question answer.
6. For vocabulary, predict sentence meaning before using word familiarity.
7. Move on when the evidence is sufficient; do not reread the whole passage to resolve a local uncertainty.

## Review habit

For every missed or tempting question, record: the predicted claim; the exact supporting sentence; the tempting choice; and the smallest word or logical relationship that disqualifies it. This creates a diagnostic catalogue of traps rather than a list of memorized answers.

## Scope note

These are instructor strategies from unofficial video sources, not College Board rules. Treat them as procedures to test against hard official questions.
